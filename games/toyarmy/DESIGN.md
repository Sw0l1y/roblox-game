## Art bible
- **Palette:** floor (honey wood) `#E2B276`, walls (sky wallpaper) `#AED4F0`, Green Army `#48B43E`, Tan Army `#E0BA72`, rare gold `#FFC62C`, UI ink `#1C1E30` (+ toy accents red `#EC4842`, blue `#488EEC`, yellow `#FFD03E`, purple `#A070EC`).
- **Lighting:** preset **B** (warm interior), tuned: ClockTime 16.2 (low sun through the window → sunbeams across the floor), Brightness 2.6, Exposure +0.15, Ambient `#967E76`, OutdoorAmbient `#B4988A`, Atmosphere density 0.22 / haze 1.2 in warm peach `#FFD8B6`, clouds 0.5, CC +0.03 brightness / 0.1 contrast / 0.18 saturation with a warm tint. Re-applied by the server via `Look.apply`.
- **World:** one giant kid's bedroom (528 × 448 studs, a toy soldier is ~4 studs): bed with a striped blanket (the cat sleeps on it), round rug in the middle, toy chest under the window, desk with lamp and pencils, bookshelf, dresser with a rocket, letter-block tower by the door, scattered toys (balls, crayons, a toy car, a xylophone), bunting and a clock on the walls. Outside the window: a sunlit lawn, trees and hills. Zones sit on giant bottle caps.
- **Mesh sources:** everything is our own primitives (SmoothPlastic is the look: the toys ARE plastic). No toolbox or catalog ids.
- **Hero assets (`Config.Meshes`, AI-mesh slots with primitive fallbacks):** Rifleman, Bazooka, Medic, Tank, Heli, General, Dino (single-colour plastic toys: no texture, the part colour becomes the team colour) and the house Cat (35-part primitive cat with jointed legs, tail, head and blinking eyes until the mesh is generated).
- **Materials (`materials.json`):** ToyFloorWood (WoodPlanks), ToyRugCarpet (Carpet, tinted per ring), ToyBlanket (Fabric, tinted). Kit HouseGrass for the lawn outside. All have a flat colour fallback.
- **UI:** kit UI (LuckiestGuy titles, chunky strokes), emoji glyphs from `Config.Icons`, tier gradients from `Tiers`.
