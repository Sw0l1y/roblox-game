# Build the Rocket! 🚀

**Tagline:** Haul giant rocket parts with your crew, bolt them on, and launch the whole server to the Moon, Mars and beyond.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core verb** | Haul (lift, carry together, bolt on) |
| **Server size** | 30 |
| **Maturity target** | Minimal |
| **Session loop (30 s)** | Grab a part at the depot (or the glowing pad by spawn) → carry it over your heads to the glowing ring on the launch pad (speed = total strength of everyone holding it ÷ its weight) → it flies up and bolts onto the rocket → coins, permanent strength, an Index entry and a spot on the crew board. |
| **Progress loop (1 day)** | Spend coins on Gear: Power Gloves, Space Suit and Jetpack multiply strength (the jetpack also raises jumps), and Drone Crew mines coins every minute, offline too. The 7-day login streak and the daily Fuel Run quest (bolt on 10 parts for coins and a 15 min 3x boost). Rank up from Cadet to Legend by lifetime weight hauled (+5% strength per rank). |
| **Meta loop (28 days)** | Planet chain Earth → Moon → Mars → Europa, then the chain repeats as Mission 2, 3… with +50% coins per mission. Each planet has its own gravity, part weights (x2.5 / x6 / x15), coin rates and a bigger rocket. Index of 8 parts × 7 rarities (+1% strength each). Rocket skins unlocked by launches and planets, or bought. Weekly new planet (see live-ops). |
| **Public display** | One giant rocket in the middle of every planet that grows slot by slot (holograms turn solid in the deliverer's rarity colours), a progress billboard over it, the crew board (in the world and on the HUD), rank tags over every head, and the top hauler's skin painting the server rocket. The MVP rides the nose at launch, VIPs get the gold window balcony. |
| **Social hook** | Team lift: heavy parts (boosters, tanks) are too heavy for one new player, so you tap **NEED A HAND!** and other players (and crew bots) grab on; everyone holding it is paid by their share of strength. Gloves and passes let veterans carry newbies' parts. |
| **Clip moment** | The launch: last part bolts on → "ALL ABOARD!" → everyone is seated on the rocket (MVP on the nose, VIPs on the gold balcony) → 10-second countdown with an orbiting camera and building smoke → liftoff with fire plumes, smoke billows, camera shake, clouds rushing past and the sky darkening into space → white warp → a lander touches down and the server arrives on the Moon in low gravity. |
| **FTUE** | 0-10 s: spawn facing the half-built rocket; a glowing, sparkling Nose Cone bobs on a gold pad at your feet (just beside the path, so the rocket stays in view) with a LIFT prompt, a gold beam and arrow point at it. 30 s: it bolts on with a fly-up animation, coin fly and strength popup; beam now points to the depot. 60 s: the depot hands a new player a free **Epic** part (gift banner, rare beam); first heavy part auto-sends a "Need a hand!" ping and crew bots come to help. ~4 min: the starter rocket (8 open slots in solo) is complete and launches; landing summary + new planet. Clear next goal on screen the whole time (beam + hint line, Gear button pulses when gloves are affordable). No purchase offers before minute 3. |

## Art bible

- **Palette (5):** launch-pad grey `#B0B8C6`, safety orange `#FF8424`, sky blue `#5AC8FF`, white `#F2F0E8`, rare gold `#FFC428` (plus navy ink `#263054` for trim and hologram cyan `#46E1FF` for glow).
- **Lighting:** Earth uses preset **A** (bright cartoon day, clouds on). Planets use preset **D** (space) with coloured Atmosphere haze per planet: Moon violet, Mars peach-red, Europa ice blue. The flight darkens the sky locally with a camera ColorCorrection.
- **Materials:** SmoothPlastic palette parts everywhere. `HouseSlabs` on the launch pad and landing pads, `HouseGrass` / `HouseSand` on the Earth island, and 3 planet grounds still to generate (`materials.json`: MoonDust, MarsSoil, EuropaIce, near-white maps tinted by the ground colour). Until generated, everything is flat palette colour.
- **Mesh sources:** our own chunky palette primitives for every rocket stage (engine, tanks, crew decks, boosters, booster caps, fins, capsule, nose), scenery (towers, crane, depot, kiosks, domes, rover, mesas, ice spikes) and crew bots. No kit or store models.
- **Hero assets (Config.Meshes, AI later, primitive fallback ships):** `noseCone` (white cone, orange tip and rings), `crewBot` (round white robot, cyan visor), `supplyPod` (white drop pod, orange stripes, three legs).
- **Landmark:** the rocket on its pad with the launch tower and crane, visible from spawn; spawn faces it down a lamp-lit path.
